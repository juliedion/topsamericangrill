Our story

One family, one corner, three buildings.

“We focus on quality, and we usually get it right, and if we don't get it right, we make it right.”

Evan Christou, third-generation owner
  1. 1930s
    The Sodium Diner

    A converted rail car opens at the Five Corners in Rotterdam.

  2. 1953
    The family takes over

    The Christou family buys the diner and names it Tops, after the New Jersey diner where a relative once worked.

  3. 1960
    A new diner car

    A modular diner replaces the old rail car and serves Rotterdam for 46 years.

  4. 1969
    The second generation

    Brothers Nick, Jim and Evan's father take over the business together.

  5. 1987
    Evan joins

    Evan Christou starts in the family business. He runs it today.

  6. 2006
    Tops American Grill, Bakery & Bar

    The building you see today goes up on the same lot, with a full bakery and bar.
